About PARITEK-UGANDA

A real game shop in central Kampala

PARITEK-UGANDA is a gaming shop on Kampala Road, located inside EM Plaza on Level 3, Shop 509. Its own public listings describe a dealer in video games — buying and selling consoles, games and accessories, fixing controllers and game consoles, and accepting console trade-ins.

The shop posts its current stock openly on TikTok and Instagram, and runs an official store on Jiji.ug, one of Uganda’s largest online marketplaces. This website brings that public information together in one place, so you can find the shop, browse recent listings, and confirm exactly what is in stock by phone or WhatsApp before you visit.
